Hi, I'm Rich! A 22-year-old self-taught designer from Brooklyn. I was always the kid taking things apart and putting them back together, from bikes to cameras to engines. At 18 I fell in love with making clothes. It started with just what I wanted to wear, but grew into art. A way to push back on an increasingly online world and, hopefully, get people outside.

The video above is the whole story from how I taught myself to make clothes to showing them at 11 fashion shows in New York, London, and Paris, and ending up making five pieces for KidSuper after meeting Colm at the 2025 People's Runway. Feel free to take a look at my clothes and portfolio and reach me on Instagram or at richard9kaulinsh@gmail.com.
