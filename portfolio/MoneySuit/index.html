Million Dollar Suit

The Million Dollar Suit is a full-body statement layered in hundreds of custom “One Million Dollar” bills, each stitched down to flap and fold with every movement. Built on a tailored white base, the suit transforms currency into armor — absurd, excessive, and unmistakably loud. Bills are printed with the phrase “feed the rich,” twisting the fantasy of wealth into something theatrical and surreal. As the bills peel outward, they expose the emptiness behind them, turning the wearer into a walking contradiction dressed as success.
